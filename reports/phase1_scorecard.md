# Phase 1 Scorecard — Honest, Leakage-Free Evaluation

_Generated by `scripts/evaluate.py` · 4 rolling-origin folds · horizons [1, 6, 24, 72]h · 100 MW facility._

## How to read this
- **mean pinball** — the proper accuracy metric for a quantile forecast (lower is better). Replaces the old MAE-only number.
- **interval cov.** — how often the realized spread fell inside the p10–p90 band. Nominal target is **80%**; far from it = miscalibrated uncertainty.
- **capture rate** — share of the theoretically available arbitrage value (perfect foresight) that following this forecast's GENERATE/IMPORT signal actually banks. This is the dollars metric.
- **savings** — modeled value over a 90-day-ish window vs. always buying from the grid. Perfect-foresight ceiling ≈ $19,189,049.

## The point
The strongest model by pinball is **LightGBM (refit/fold)**. The bar it had to clear is the persistence baseline — *no model ships unless it beats persistence on this leakage-free backtest.* The capture-rate column translates accuracy into the only thing a customer cares about: dollars of arbitrage actually banked.

> Note on the *old* numbers: the previous `train_spread_forecaster.py` reported MAE from a split that grouped rows by site before slicing, leaking overlapping future timestamps into training. Those figures were optimistic; everything above is measured with strictly-past training and an embargo ≥ the max horizon.
